1. The Structured Problem-Solving Cycle (Whiteboard Anchor)

Keep this 4-step framework written on the corner of your whiteboard to guide every exercise across all topics:
1. Understand (Requirements): Identify the inputs, constraints, and exact goal. Rewrite the problem in your own words or draw a quick diagram.
2. Plan (Patterns & Logic): Look for recurring patterns, break the problem into smaller sub-problems, or choose a mathematical formula/conversion method.
3. Execute (Paper & Worksheets): Work through the steps cleanly on paper without skipping intermediate states (especially when carrying bits or tracking logic rules).
4. Verify (Review): Test the answer against edge cases, reverse the math (e.g., convert binary back to decimal), and check if every initial requirement was met.
